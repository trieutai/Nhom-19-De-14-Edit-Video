DUKTAY EDIT - CAP NHAT XOA VIDEO, DANG NHAP VA CACH LY TAI KHOAN

1. SAO LUU TRUOC KHI CAP NHAT:
   - Sao chep C:\xampp\htdocs\video_tool ra cho an toan.
   - Xuat backup database VideoToolDB bang phpMyAdmin (Export -> SQL).
   - Backup thu muc uploads va outputs (chua video that).

2. CHI GHI DE CAC FILE DUOC DONG GOI TRONG ZIP NAY:
   - Giai nen vao C:\xampp\htdocs\video_tool, giu nguyen cau truc con.
   - Chu y: co 2 file uploads\.htaccess va outputs\.htaccess; Windows can hien file an neu can.
   - Khong xoa thu muc uploads/ va outputs/ dang co; khong xoa video cu.
   - Giu nguyen VideoProcessor.php va cac file khac khong co trong ZIP.

3. MO XAMPP:
   - Bat Apache va MySQL/MariaDB.
   - Mo http://localhost:8080/video_tool/index.php (doi cong neu may su dung cong khac).
   - Lan dau vao login.php, tao tai khoan email va mat khau >=8 ky tu.
   - db.php se them bang users/images/image_outputs va cot user_id neu chua co.
   - Tai video moi de test, bam "Cong viec" -> "Chay".

4. NHUNG THAY DOI:
   - Trang chu co nut Dang nhap/Dang ky va Dang xuat.
   - Bang cong viec co nut Xoa (xoa job + video xuat cua job, KHONG xoa nguon).
   - Man hinh Video co "Xoa video nguon" (xoa nguon + tat ca job/video xuat lien quan).
   - Khong xoa khi job dang processing: Huy, doi worker dung roi moi Xoa.
   - Moi tai khoan chi duoc xem jobs/videos cua minh. uploads/user_ID va outputs/user_ID.
   - media.php kiem tra tai khoan truoc khi phuc vu anh/video/download.
   - uploads/.htaccess va outputs/.htaccess chan URL truy cap truc tiep.
   - Nhanh image_editor cung phai dang nhap, upload/crop theo tai khoan.

5. DU LIEU CU:
   - Du lieu videos/jobs da ton tai truoc nang cap KHONG duoc tu dong gan cho bat ky tai khoan nao.
   - Chung van nam trong database va thu muc cu, nhung khong hien thi cho tai khoan moi.
   - CAN du lieu cu: sao luu va xac minh chu so huu truoc khi chuyen du lieu, KHONG tu sua user_id vo dieu kien.

6. KIEM TRA BAO MAT BAT BUOC TREN MAY XAMPP:
   a) Tao hai tai khoan A va B, dang A upload 1 video, cat xuat.
   b) Dang xuat, dang B: khong thay video/job cua A.
   c) Voi B, truy cap URL media.php?type=source&id=<ID cua A> => phai nhan HTTP 404.
   d) Thu mo URL truc tiep http://localhost:8080/video_tool/uploads/<tenfilecu>.mp4
      => PHAI nhan HTTP 403. Lam tuong tu voi /outputs/.
      Neu truy cap duoc 200 thi Apache chua nap .htaccess / AllowOverride:
      PHAI cau hinh Apache chan uploads va outputs truoc khi su dung nhieu tai khoan.
   e) B khong duoc huy/xoa/chay job cua A (POST + CSRF hop le cua B).
   f) Dang A xoa 1 job: file output va thumbnail phai bien mat, video nguon con nguyen.
   g) Dang A xoa video nguon: video nguon, thumbnail, tat ca job va ket qua lien quan bien mat.
   h) Dang A/B cap nhat lai trang va khoi dong lai XAMPP de kiem tra dang nhap.

7. GIOI HAN KIEM TRA:
   - Da chay 'php -l' cho tat ca file PHP trong goi: khong co loi cu phap.
   - CHUA duoc thu truc tiep voi MySQL/MariaDB va Apache cua ban. Phai test muc 6.
   - KHONG duoc dua len internet cong khai truoc khi kiem tra .htaccess/HTTPS, gioi han upload,
     va thuc hien danh gia an toan trien khai.
