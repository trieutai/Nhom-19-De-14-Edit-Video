VIDEO TOOL - NANG CAP DE TAI 14 (PHP + XAMPP)
=========================================

1. SAO LUU TRUOC KHI CAP NHAT
- Sao luu database VideoToolDB bang phpMyAdmin (Export -> SQL).
- Sao luu cac file PHP cu.
- KHONG XOA thu muc uploads/ va outputs/: day la video goc va ket qua cu.

2. COPY CHI CAC FILE CO THAY DOI VAO
  C:\xampp\htdocs\video_tool\
- video_editor.php: giao dien, kiem tra tham so, xep hang doi, huy/thu lai
- db.php: ket noi va nang cap bang hien co (khong xoa du lieu)
- VideoProcessor.php: FFmpeg, FFprobe, kiem tra dau ra
- process_worker.php: tien trinh hang doi PHP CLI
- upload_temp.php: tai anh an toan
- image_editor.php: gioi han duong dan va tham so cat anh
- test_de_tai_14.php: bo thu tu dong (file moi)
Giu nguyen cac file khac, dac biet preview_frame.php, video_quality.php neu dang su dung.

3. KHOI DONG XAMPP
- Bat Apache va MySQL.
- Bao dam FFmpeg va FFprobe chay duoc trong terminal bang: ffmpeg -version; ffprobe -version.
- Neu PHP khong tim thay FFmpeg, them duong dan thu muc bin cua FFmpeg vao PATH, sau do khoi dong lai Apache va terminal.
- PHP 8.0+ la can thiet. Bat PDO_MySQL va cac ham proc_open, shell_exec, exec neu hosting chan.
- php.ini: upload_max_filesize=512M, post_max_size=520M, max_file_uploads=20 (sau do restart Apache).
- Truy cap: http://localhost:8080/video_tool/video_editor.php (hay dung cong Apache thuc te).
- Lan dau mo video_editor.php, db.php se bo sung cac cot can thiet trong MySQL.

4. BAT WORKER - BAT BUOC
Mo CMD (giu cua so nay mo trong khi xu ly video):
  C:\xampp\php\php.exe C:\xampp\htdocs\video_tool\process_worker.php
Worker tu lay job trang thai pending tu MySQL va chay FFmpeg; trang web khong xu ly video truc tiep nua.
De xu ly toi da mot job roi thoat:
  C:\xampp\php\php.exe C:\xampp\htdocs\video_tool\process_worker.php --once
Khi tao job: web hien Cho xu ly; worker tu lay job; bam "Cap nhat" de xem trang thai.
Dung Ctrl+C de dung worker. Neu worker bi mat ket noi/dung dot ngot, job khong co heartbeat tren 120s se duoc thu lai sau khi worker moi khoi dong.

5. HUONG DAN THU NGHIEM
  C:\xampp\php\php.exe C:\xampp\htdocs\video_tool\test_de_tai_14.php
Bo test tu dong tao video tam 30s, kiem tra 8 tinh huong FFmpeg, sau do don tep tam.
Test thu cong tren XAMPP:
- Tai 1 video hop le + 1 video hong; xac nhan video hong bi tu choi.
- Cat 5..20 giay; xem trang thai pending -> processing -> completed.
- Tai file, do duration, width, height va audio; giao dien hien thong so da do.
- Huy job pending va job dang processing; kiem tra khong tao output hoan tat.
- Thu lai job error/cancelled; kiem tra job_attempts co so lan xu ly moi va tep nguon giu nguyen.
- Mo 2 cua so worker cung luc; kiem tra moi job chi duoc nhan 1 lan.
- Kiem tra metadata va file dau ra bang ffprobe.

6. GIOI HAN VA LUU Y
- Ket qua: bo thu FFmpeg 8/8 da chay trong moi truong phat trien; chua thu truc tiep database MySQL/XAMPP tren may cua ban.
- Ung dung chua co he thong dang nhap, phan quyen vai tro. Chay chi tren localhost/LAN tin cay; KHONG cong khai len Internet truoc khi bo sung xac thuc, quyen truy cap va chinh sach quan ly tep.
- Endpoint preview_frame.php va video_quality.php chua duoc nang cap trong dot nay; can gioi han tai nguyen va quyen truy cap truoc khi dua len mang cong khai.
- Viec cat dung noi dung duoc thuc hien qua FFmpeg tai moc nhap; sai so do timestamp/frame phu thuoc FPS. Dung dung sai <= max(0.35s, min(0.75s, 2% do dai doan)).
- Neu MySQL cu co job trang thai 'processing' do code cu de lai, can xem xet va doi ve pending sau khi dam bao khong con worker cu hoat dong.
